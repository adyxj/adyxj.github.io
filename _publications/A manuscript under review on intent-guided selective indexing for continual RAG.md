**Status:** Under review.

**Summary.** Most RAG pipelines index every incoming document without regard to whether it will ever be retrieved. SCOPE instead uses a user&rsquo;s evolving intent to
decide what enters the index, filtering low-value content before it incurs retrieval cost. This keeps retrieval accurate and efficient under long, continuously growing
document streams.
