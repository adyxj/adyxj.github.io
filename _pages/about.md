I am a graduate student at [Harbin Institute of Technology](https://www.hit.edu.cn/). My research lies in retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), with a focus on
**graph-based retrieval (GraphRAG)** and **personalized, continual retrieval**.

A central challenge I work on is that real retrieval systems face long, continuously growing document streams, yet most indexing pipelines treat every document as
equally worth retrieving. I study how a user's evolving information intent can guide *what gets indexed in the first place*—filtering out content that is unlikely to
ever be relevant before it enters the index—so that retrieval stays both accurate and efficient over time.

I am currently working on a manuscript, *SCOPE: Intent-Guided Selective Indexing for Continual Retrieval-Augmented Generation*, which is under review.

## Research Interests

- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
- Graph-based Retrieval (GraphRAG)
- Personalized and Continual Retrieval
- Intent Modeling for Information Filtering
